## Internship Overview

This internship provided exposure to beverage manufacturing, production-line operations, packaging, compressed-air systems, steam utilities, and quality control at the Multan beverage unit.

My internship reports refer to the organization as **Pepsi-Shamim & Co. (Pvt.) Limited**. The completion letter was issued by **JK Sugar Mills (Pvt.) Limited, Pepsi Cola Bottlers**.

The experience connected industrial engineering coursework with the operation of automated and semi-automated production systems.

## Work and Analysis

- Observed the production flow from beverage preparation and bottle forming through filling, packaging, and storage.
- Studied the relationship between utility availability and production-line continuity.
- Explored air-demand estimation for different PET bottle sizes as an academic calculation exercise.
- Reviewed steam-use patterns and direct and indirect boiler-efficiency assessment concepts.
- Documented technical observations on compressors, boilers, conveyors, and packaging equipment.
- Developed recommendations concerning preventive maintenance, utility-use monitoring, and equipment reliability.

## Improvement Themes Explored

- Preventive maintenance and routine equipment inspection.
- Compressed-air leak monitoring and utility-use tracking.
- Conveyor and sensor reliability.
- Steam-demand monitoring and energy-efficiency opportunities.
- Operator awareness, quality checks, and continuous improvement.
