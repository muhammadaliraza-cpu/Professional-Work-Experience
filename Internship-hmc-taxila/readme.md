# Industrial Engineering Internship at Heavy Mechanical Complex

## Muhammad Ali Raza

**Heavy Mechanical Complex (HMC), Taxila, Pakistan**

## Internship Overview

This internship provided exposure to production planning, heavy manufacturing, material management, and quality assurance at HMC, Taxila.

Through departmental visits, shop-floor observations, and technical documentation, I studied how engineering drawings, materials, manufacturing operations, inspection, and dispatch are coordinated within a heavy engineering organization.

## Technical Exposure

| Area | Topics Studied and Observed |
|---|---|
| Production Planning & Material Management | Order processing, material requirements, stock availability, inventory coding, and job dispatching |
| Design & Technical Planning | Engineering drawings, shop routing, and coordination between design and production |
| Foundry & Pattern Making | Sand molding, core boxes, casting processes, pattern allowances, and material checks |
| Fabrication | Plate cutting, welding, rolling, forming, and bending operations |
| Forging & Heat Treatment | Open-die and closed-die forging, hardening, tempering, annealing, and stress relief |
| Machining & Assembly | Turning, milling, boring, gear hobbing, grinding, and component assembly |
| Inspection & Quality Assurance | Dimensional inspection, material testing, non-destructive testing methods, and inspection documentation |

## Key Activities and Learning

- Studied the connection between customer orders, engineering drawings, material planning, manufacturing, and dispatch.
- Examined how production planning coordinates work across multiple manufacturing shops.
- Observed the manufacturing stages used to produce heavy industrial components.
- Reviewed inspection and test plans, quality control records, and dimensional inspection practices.
- Documented manufacturing processes, equipment functions, and departmental workflows in an internship report.
